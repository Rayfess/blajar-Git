\# PRD — E-Commerce Application



\## 1. Product Context



\### Problem



Customers need a convenient way to discover products,

place orders, and track their purchases.



Sellers need a way to manage products and fulfill orders.



\### Goal



Provide a platform where customers can purchase products

and sellers can manage their products and orders.



\### Target Users



\- Customer

\- Seller

\- Administrator



\---



\## 2. Scope



\### In Scope



\- User registration and authentication

\- Product browsing

\- Product search

\- Product details

\- Shopping cart

\- Checkout

\- Order creation

\- Order tracking

\- Seller product management

\- Seller order management

\- Administrative management



\### Out of Scope



\- Physical delivery operations

\- Manufacturing

\- Accounting system

\- External marketplace synchronization
